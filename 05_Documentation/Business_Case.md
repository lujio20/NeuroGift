# NeuroGift — Business Case

## One-Line Pitch

**NeuroGift reads your brain to teach you the way you actually learn — not the way everyone else does.**

---

## The Problem (Market Size)

- Global EdTech market: **$340B by 2025**, growing at 16.3% CAGR
- 1.5 billion students worldwide use digital learning platforms
- **89% of students** report that online courses don't match how they learn (self-reported)
- Current "personalization" is based on what you clicked, not how your brain works

**The gap:** No product today uses biological signals to personalize learning. NeuroGift fills this gap.

---

## The Solution

**NeuroGift = EEG Sensing + AI Classification + Smart Content Delivery**

In 3 steps:
1. Wear the EEG headset for 2 minutes
2. Answer 3 questions
3. Get a personalized learning feed — forever

---

## Target Market

### Primary
- **University students** (18–26) using online learning platforms
- Early adopters of EdTech and wearable technology

### Secondary
- **Corporate training departments** — onboard employees faster by matching training to their learning style
- **K-12 institutions** — support teachers in adapting content to different learner types
- **EdTech platforms** (Coursera, Udemy, etc.) — license the API to enhance their recommendation systems

### Geography (Phase 1)
- Saudi Arabia + GCC (aligned with Vision 2030 education initiatives)
- Expansion to MENA, then global

---

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | What They Do | NeuroGift Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Coursera | Course platform with basic recommendations | No biological personalization |
| Khan Academy | Adaptive learning via quiz performance | Reactive, not proactive |
| Neurable | EEG for gaming/productivity | Not focused on education |
| MindMaze | EEG for medical/rehab | Completely different domain |
| VARK / Learning Style Tests | Questionnaire-based typing | Subjective, inaccurate |

**No direct competitor combines EEG + AI + educational content transformation.**
